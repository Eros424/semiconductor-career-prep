# Keyword-led STAR structure

## 01 · Start here

- From research to engineering
- One method: change one factor, observe the response, make the next step clear

## Case 01 · Overflow STAR

### 02 · Task

- Change the packing structure
- Keep flow and other conditions fixed
- Observe when overflow appears

### 03 · Action

- Observe
- Identify
- Collect data
- Verification

### 04 · Result

- Overflow appeared when packing specific surface area became larger
- Liquid flow was adjusted in the same overflow-prone experiment
- Overflow stopped
- Flow became a control variable for later comparison
