# RPB Engineering Portfolio｜Presentation Speaker Script

## 使用方式

- Presentation Mode 共 7 頁，建議總長約 5 分鐘。
- 每頁先講英文，再依需要補充中文。
- 右側 keyword 是提示順序，不需要逐字朗讀。
- `Conceptual` 圖像與尚未確認的量測資料不作為口頭證據。

## 01｜Start here｜約 30 秒

### English

Good morning. My background is in chemical engineering. During my master’s research, I studied mass transfer in a rotating packed bed, or RPB.

This portfolio shows two short STAR cases. The first is an overflow problem. The second is how I shared what I learned and helped a project student complete the work.

My working method is simple. I change one factor, observe the response, and make the next step clear.

### 中文

各位主管好，我的背景是化學工程。碩士研究期間，我研究旋轉填充床，也就是 RPB 的質傳性能。

這份作品集有兩個簡短的 STAR 案例。第一個是 RPB 溢流問題，第二個是我如何分享經驗，協助專題生完成研究。

我的工作方法很簡單。先改變一個因素，觀察設備反應，再釐清下一步。

---

## 02｜Overflow · Situation + Task｜約 40 秒

### English

My research compared different packing structures under controlled operating conditions. I first changed the packing structure while keeping the flow and other conditions fixed.

The task was to understand why the same system behaved differently. When the packing specific surface area became larger, overflow appeared. Liquid came out from the top of the equipment, so the operating condition was no longer stable enough for a reliable comparison.

### 中文

我的研究是在控制操作條件下，比較不同的填料結構。

第一步，我先改變填料結構，固定流量和其他條件，觀察設備反應。

我發現當填料比表面積變大時，設備出現溢流，液體從上方跑出來。這代表設備狀態不夠穩定，後續數據也不容易可靠比較。

---

## 03｜Overflow · Action｜約 70 秒

### English

I used four simple steps.

First, I observed the system while keeping the original conditions fixed. Second, I identified the packing structure as the main changed factor. Third, I collected the operating information and compared the overflow condition.

To verify the possible cause, I used the same experiment that had overflowed. I first checked that the setup and connections were in place. Then I adjusted the liquid flow step by step and observed the result.

### 中文

我用四個簡單步驟排查。

第一是觀察，在原本條件固定的情況下看設備怎麼變化。第二是辨識，把填料結構視為主要改變因素。第三是整理資料，比較發生溢流時的操作條件。

為了驗證可能原因，我回到同樣會溢流的實驗。先確認設備組裝和管線狀態，再逐步調整液體流量，觀察結果。

---

## 04｜Overflow · Result｜約 40 秒

### English

After I adjusted the liquid flow, the overflow stopped in the same overflow-prone experiment. This gave me a more stable operating range for later work.

Afterward, I reviewed the literature. I learned that liquid flow and overall mass-transfer efficiency were not simply the same thing. I therefore treated liquid flow as a control variable and continued the experiments under a more stable condition.

I can confirm that the operating condition became more stable. I do not claim that I directly proved one single root cause, because I did not directly measure pressure drop or flow distribution.

### 中文

調整液體流量後，在原本容易溢流的實驗中，溢流消失了。這讓我找到一個比較穩定的操作範圍，能繼續後面的實驗。

事後我查詢文獻，了解到液體流量和整體質傳效率不一定是簡單的正相關。因此，我把流量當作控制變因，在比較穩定的條件下繼續研究。

我可以確認操作狀態變得比較穩定，但不會宣稱已經直接證明唯一的根本原因，因為當時沒有直接量測壓降或流量分布。

---

## 05｜Team · Situation + Task｜約 35 秒

### English

This overflow experience also became useful when I worked with a project student. We shared the same RPB, but we had different research tasks and limited equipment time.

The task was to share the finding, organize the setup, and keep the student’s research moving without losing the experimental sequence.

### 中文

這次溢流經驗，也成為我協助專題生時可以分享的資訊。

我們共用同一台 RPB，但有不同的研究任務，也受到設備時間限制。

當時的任務，是分享我發現的溢流現象、協助整理實驗組裝，讓專題生可以按照順序繼續研究。

---

## 06｜Team · Action｜約 55 秒

### English

I shared the overflow information and explained what I had checked. I helped with the experimental assembly and explained the operating sequence in a practical way.

I also coordinated the shared equipment around the two research tasks. When the next step was unclear, I helped break it into a smaller action and confirmed what needed to happen next.

My role was to provide structure, equipment guidance, and coordination. I did not take ownership of the student’s research conclusions.

### 中文

我先分享溢流的資訊，說明自己檢查過哪些地方。接著協助實驗組裝，也用比較實際的方式說明設備操作順序。

因為兩個研究任務共用同一台設備，我也協助安排使用順序。當下一步不清楚時，就把它拆成較小的行動，確認接下來要做什麼。

我的角色是提供研究架構、設備操作協助與進度協調。我沒有把專題生的研究結論說成自己的成果。

---

## 07｜Team · Result｜約 45 秒

### English

As a result, the project student completed the thesis project and later received a competition award.

That result belongs to the student. My contribution was the structure around the work: sharing the overflow finding, helping with the equipment setup, explaining the operation, and coordinating the shared resource.

This is also how I would contribute as an Equipment Engineer. I would make the equipment state clear, use evidence before conclusions, follow safety rules and SOPs, document the result, and escalate issues beyond my authorization.

Thank you.

### 中文

最後，專題生完成了論文，也在後續競賽中獲得獎項。

這個成果屬於專題生。我提供的貢獻，是研究工作的結構，包括分享溢流發現、協助設備組裝、說明操作方式，以及協調共用設備。

未來如果擔任設備工程師，我會用同樣的方法工作。先讓設備狀態清楚，再用證據判斷，遵守安全規範與 SOP，記錄結果，並把超出授權範圍的問題依程序回報。

Thank you.
