# Reference interaction research

Retrieved 2026-11-18 (Asia/Taipei). The available web reader returned page text and structure. It did not provide an authorized interactive browser session for drag, scroll, or mobile behavior, so those behaviors are recorded as design principles rather than source-site test results.

| Source | Observed principle | Applied in prototype |
| --- | --- | --- |
| https://ciechanow.ski/mechanical-watch/ | Drag or slide to reveal internal mechanisms, color-coded parts, and explanations that stay synchronized with the visual. | Compact RPB system view and state-led explanation, without adding a dense equipment legend. |
| https://pudding.cool/ | A digital publication organized around story entries and data-led editorial pacing. | Seven full-height chapters with a chapter rail and scroll-driven reveal. |
| https://observablehq.com/@d3/-/collection/gallery | Reusable interactive chart patterns including line, bar, tree, tooltip, and zoom examples. | Result buttons and keyword cards that reveal one evidence statement at a time. |

## Design limits

No source images, confirmed RPB engineering drawing, original microscopy image, or complete measurement table was available in the project folder for this first version. Those areas remain explicitly replaceable and labelled as conceptual or demo content.
